## Covered Services

Preventive care, including annual wellness visits, immunizations, and routine
screenings, is covered at 100% with no cost-sharing when received from an
in-network provider. Primary care office visits are subject to the plan's
copay as listed on your plan summary. Specialist visits require a referral
under HMO plans but not under PPO or EPO plans.

Emergency room visits are covered at all plans regardless of network status,
though out-of-network facilities may bill separately for services beyond
what the plan allows. Urgent care is covered at a lower copay than the ER
and is the recommended first stop for non-life-threatening issues outside
normal office hours.

Inpatient hospital stays, including surgery, anesthesia, and a semi-private
room, are covered after the deductible is met, subject to coinsurance.
Prior authorization is required for all non-emergency inpatient admissions;
failure to obtain authorization may reduce the benefit paid by up to 50%.

Outpatient diagnostic services such as lab work, X-rays, and basic imaging
(ultrasound, standard X-ray) are covered under the plan's standard
coinsurance. Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET scans) requires prior
authorization and is subject to a separate imaging copay on some plans.

Prescription drug coverage follows a four-tier formulary: generic,
preferred brand, non-preferred brand, and specialty. Mail-order pharmacy
is available for 90-day supplies of maintenance medications at a reduced
copay compared to retail pharmacy fills.

Mental health and substance use disorder services are covered at parity
with medical and surgical benefits, including outpatient therapy,
intensive outpatient programs, and inpatient behavioral health admissions.

Maternity care, including prenatal visits, delivery, and postpartum care,
is covered as a standard medical benefit with no separate waiting period.
Newborn care is covered under the mother's plan for the first 31 days of
life, after which the newborn must be separately enrolled.

## Exclusions

The following services are not covered under any plan unless specifically
noted as a rider on your plan summary: cosmetic surgery that is not
reconstructive following an accident or mastectomy; elective bariatric
surgery unless medical necessity criteria are met and pre-approved;
fertility treatments including IVF, unless purchased as an optional rider;
long-term custodial care in a nursing facility; routine dental and vision
care (available only through separate dental/vision riders); acupuncture
and other services deemed experimental or investigational by the plan's
medical policy committee.

Services received from a provider who has opted out of the plan's network
without prior authorization are excluded from coverage except in a true
emergency as defined by the prudent layperson standard. Claims for
services rendered outside the United States are excluded except for
emergency stabilization while traveling, which is covered up to the
out-of-network benefit level.

Weight-loss programs, non-prescription nutritional supplements, and
over-the-counter medications are excluded from the medical benefit, though
some may be reimbursable under a linked flexible spending account.
Injuries sustained while committing a felony, and any service for which
no charge would be made in the absence of insurance, are also excluded.

## Member Cost-Sharing Summary

Deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and out-of-network penalties vary by
plan tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) as detailed in each plan's
individual summary of benefits. Members should always confirm current
cost-sharing amounts through the member portal before scheduling non-
emergency services, since amounts are subject to change at renewal.
