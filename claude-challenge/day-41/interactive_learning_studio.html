Summary Notes

  • Classes bundle data (attributes) and behavior (methods) together.
  • Encapsulation hides internal state behind a controlled interface.
  • Inheritance lets subclasses reuse and extend parent behavior.
  • Polymorphism lets different classes respond to the same method call in their own way.
  • Dunder methods let custom objects integrate with Python's built-in syntax (printing, equality, operators).
