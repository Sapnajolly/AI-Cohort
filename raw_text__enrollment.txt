## Enrollment

New members may enroll during the annual open enrollment period, which
runs from November 1 through December 15 for coverage effective January 1
of the following year. Coverage elected during open enrollment cannot be
changed until the next open enrollment period unless the member
experiences a qualifying life event.

Qualifying life events that trigger a special enrollment period include:
marriage or divorce, birth or adoption of a child, loss of other health
coverage (such as job loss or aging off a parent's plan), a permanent
move to a new coverage area, and gaining or losing eligibility for
Medicaid or CHIP. Members have 60 days from the qualifying event to enroll
or make changes to their existing coverage.

To enroll, members submit an application through the employer portal (for
employer-sponsored plans) or the individual marketplace (for individual
plans), selecting a plan tier — Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum — based
on their expected healthcare needs and budget. Higher-tier plans generally
carry higher monthly premiums but lower deductibles and out-of-pocket
costs, while lower-tier plans favor lower premiums with higher cost-
sharing at the point of care.

## Dependent Coverage

Dependents, including spouses and children up to age 26, may be added to
a member's plan during open enrollment or within 60 days of a qualifying
life event such as marriage or birth. Proof of relationship (marriage
certificate, birth certificate) is required within 30 days of adding a
dependent, or the dependent may be retroactively removed from coverage.

Children may remain covered as dependents until age 26 regardless of
student, marital, or financial dependent status, consistent with
applicable federal requirements. Disabled dependents who are incapable of
self-support may continue coverage beyond age 26 with proof of disability
submitted before the dependent's 26th birthday.

## Effective Dates and Termination

Coverage elected during open enrollment becomes effective January 1.
Coverage elected under a special enrollment period generally becomes
effective the first day of the month following the qualifying event,
though birth, adoption, and placement for adoption are effective
retroactively to the date of the event itself.

Coverage terminates at the end of the month in which a member loses
eligibility, such as through termination of employment, divorce, or a
dependent aging out. Members may be eligible for continuation coverage
under COBRA or a state equivalent, allowing continued enrollment in the
same plan at full premium cost (plus an administrative fee) for up to 18
to 36 months depending on the qualifying event.
