Section 1: Plan Overview
The Horizon Community PPO Gold 1500 plan provides comprehensive medical, prescription drug, and
preventive care coverage for members and their eligible dependents. This plan uses a preferred
provider organization (PPO) network. Members may see any provider, but out-of-pocket costs are
lower when using in-network providers.

Section 2: Covered Services
The following services are covered under this plan when medically necessary and provided by an
in-network provider, unless otherwise noted. Prior authorization may be required for certain services
including inpatient hospital admissions, advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET scans), and durable medical
equipment over $500 in value.
- Preventive care and screenings (no cost share when in-network)
- Outpatient surgery and observation care
- Inpatient hospital stays, including maternity
- Mental health and substance use disorder services
- Rehabilitative and habilitative services (physical, occupational, speech therapy)
- Skilled nursing facility care, up to 60 days per plan year
- Home health care, up to 100 visits per plan year
- Durable medical equipment (DME)
- Diagnostic lab and imaging services
Section 3: Exclusions and Limitations
This plan does not cover cosmetic surgery unless medically necessary, long-term custodial care,
infertility treatments beyond diagnostic workup, or services provided outside the United States except in
a documented emergency. Weight loss surgery requires a 6-month physician-supervised program prior
to authorization.
Section 4: Filing a Claim
In-network providers will file claims on your behalf. If you receive care from an out-of-network provider,
you must submit a claim form within 90 days of the date of service. Claim forms are available on the
member portal at www.horizoncommunityhealth-sample.org/claims. Mail completed forms to: Horizon
Community Health Plan, PO Box 44210, Trenton, NJ 08650.
Section 5: Appeals Process
If a claim is denied, members have the right to file an internal appeal within 180 days of receiving the
denial notice. Horizon Community Health Plan will respond to standard appeals within 30 calendar days
and expedited appeals within 72 hours. If the internal appeal is denied, members may request an
independent external review through the state Department of Insurance.
